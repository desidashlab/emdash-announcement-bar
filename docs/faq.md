## What does this plugin do?

It shows one bar across the top or bottom of your site with a short message and, if you want, a link: free delivery this week, a new post, opening hours over a holiday. You write it in the admin and switch it on or off there. No theme editing after the one-time setup.

## Can I schedule it?

Yes. Set **Show from** and **Show until** and the bar appears and disappears on those days by itself. Leave either empty to skip that side. Dates follow each visitor's own clock.

## Can visitors close it?

If **Visitors can close it** is on, the bar has a close button. A visitor who closes it does not see it again on that device until you change the message or the link. Switch the option off for notices everyone must see.

## Where does the link open?

A path on your site, such as `/shop`, opens in the same tab. An address on another site opens in a new tab.

## Can I change the colours?

Yes, background and text, as hex codes such as `#111827` and `#ffffff`. The font comes from your theme.

## Does it cover my menu?

At the top the bar sits above everything else and pushes the page down, so nothing is hidden. Turn on **Stays visible while scrolling** to keep it on screen. At the bottom it floats over the page and the page gets a little extra room so the last line is never hidden.

## Does it collect anything?

No. The plugin has no access to your content and makes no requests outside your site. The only call is your site fetching its own settings.
